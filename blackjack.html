Quick strategy tips

You are not playing against the dealer. You win when your five cards make a hand on the paytable. A pair of 10s or lower pays nothing.

  • Straight or better dealt: keep all five.
  • Four to a royal flush: keep the four, even over a small pair.
  • Three of a kind, two pair, or any pair: keep them.
  • Four to a flush or open straight: keep them over a low pair or nothing.
  • Nothing: keep one or two high cards (J, Q, K, A), or draw five new cards.

Stuck? Press Hint any time during step 2 and the game shows the best cards to keep.

Side bet payouts

Lucky Lucky (your first two cards plus the dealer's up card): total 19 or 20 pays 2:1, 21 pays 3:1, suited 21 pays 15:1, 6-7-8 pays 30:1 (suited 100:1), 7-7-7 pays 50:1 (suited 200:1). Tap to cycle $0, $5, $10, $25.

Bust-It (the dealer's final hand): the dealer busts on 3 cards pays 1:1, 4 cards 2:1, 5 cards 9:1, 6 cards 50:1, 7 cards 100:1, 8 or more 250:1. If the dealer does not bust, you lose it.

Insurance is offered when the dealer shows an Ace. It costs half your bet and pays 2:1.

Play money only. Nobody is actually coming.
