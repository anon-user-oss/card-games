Perfect Pairs (your first two cards): mixed pair 6:1, colored pair 12:1, perfect pair 25:1. Tap to cycle $0, $5, $10, $25.

21+3 (your two cards plus the dealer's up card): flush 5:1, straight 10:1, three of a kind 30:1, straight flush 40:1, suited trips 100:1.

Insurance is offered when the dealer shows an Ace. It costs half your bet and pays 2:1.
